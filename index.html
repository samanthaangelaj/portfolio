    I studied Industrial Engineering & Management at KTH, specialising in Computer Science with track Interaction & Visualization, with studies abroad in Valencia and Buenos Aires. I paint surreal, cosmic and nature canvases. Somewhere between those worlds I found my practice: experiences at the edge of art, technology and physical space.
